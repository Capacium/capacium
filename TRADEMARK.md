# Capacium Trademark Policy v1alpha1

**Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Status:** PROPOSED — PENDING HUMAN OPERATOR APPROVAL  
**Custodian:** Andre Lange (Lange VC), holding in trust for the Capacium project

This document is subject to human operator approval. Do not self-approve.

## 1. Ownership

The following marks are held in trust by the current legal custodian for the
benefit of the Capacium project and its contributor community:

- "Capacium" (word mark)
- "CIP" / "Capacium Improvement Proposal" (process mark)
- Capacium-related logos, badges, and domain names including `capacium.xyz`

These marks are not owned by any individual contributor, employer, or
commercial entity. The custodian holds them pending transfer to a future
neutral foundation as defined in CHARTER.md.

## 2. Permissible Use

The following uses are permitted without prior approval:

- Identifying interoperable implementations as "compatible with Capacium"
- Describing packages as "distributed via Capacium" or "installed with Capacium"
- Referencing the Capacium name in documentation, academic work, or news coverage
- Using the marks in accordance with the project's open source licenses

## 3. Restricted Use

The following uses require explicit written approval from the custodian or
future foundation:

- Using the marks in a product, service, or company name
- Using the marks to imply formal project endorsement or certification
- Modifying or combining the marks for derivative branding
- Registering the marks or similar marks with any trademark office

## 4. Transfer Commitment

The custodian commits to transfer all trademark rights to a future neutral
foundation without encumbrance, royalty, or exclusive condition. The transfer
is documented in a public decision record.

Until transferred, the custodian may enforce the marks only to prevent:
(i) misuse that implies endorsement; and
(ii) registration by third parties.

## 5. Policy Changes

This policy may be amended only through a Governance CIP. Amendments must be
ratified by a human operator.
