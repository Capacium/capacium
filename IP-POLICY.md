# Capacium Intellectual Property Policy v1alpha1

**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** PROPOSED — PENDING HUMAN OPERATOR APPROVAL
**Canonical location:** Capacium repository root as `IP-POLICY.md`

This document is subject to human operator approval. Do not self-approve.

## 1. Scope

This policy governs IP for all Capacium project assets and binds all
contributors, maintainers, Specification Editors, and operators.

## 2. Contribution Licensing

All contributions are licensed under the project's open source licenses as
defined in `CHARTER.md` Section 6:

- Source code, tests, build scripts, and Reference Implementation: Apache
  License 2.0 (`LICENSE`).
- Specifications, normative schemas, and wire contracts: CC-BY-4.0.
- Non-normative documentation: CC-BY-4.0.
- Conformance test vectors and fixtures: Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`).

By contributing, the submitter grants recipients the rights in the applicable
license.

## 3. Developer Certificate of Origin

Every commit must include DCO sign-off:

```
Signed-off-by: Name <email>
```

Automated tooling must reject commits without DCO sign-off.

## 4. Specification Implementation License

Normative specification text, schemas, and wire contracts are made available
under CC-BY-4.0. Contributors of normative specification text grant a
royalty-free, non-exclusive, non-discriminatory patent covenant to implement
conforming portions of the specification. This covenant excludes
non-conforming implementations and combinations with unrelated technology.

## 5. IP Governance

IP-related decisions follow the CIP process (`CIP-PROCESS.md`) and charter
amendment process (`CHARTER.md` Sections 8 and 9). Licensing changes require
a CIP with Neutrality Impact Assessment and human ratification.

## 6. Trademarks

Trademarks associated with Capacium are owned by the project and must be
transferable to a future neutral foundation per `GOVERNANCE.md` Section 8.
No individual or organization may claim exclusive ownership. A trademark
usage policy is maintained in `TRADEMARK.md`.

## 7. IP Interest Disclosure

Maintainers and Specification Editors must disclose relevant IP interests in
`MAINTAINERS.md`, including relevant patents and employment relationships with
patent holders. Disclosures must be updated within 30 days of material change.

## 8. Patent-Encumbered Contributions

No contributor may knowingly submit a patent-encumbered contribution without
disclosing the encumbrance to maintainers before or at submission, identifying
the contribution, patent or application, and rights held. Incompatible
contributions must be rejected. Accepted encumbered contributions require a
public decision record.
